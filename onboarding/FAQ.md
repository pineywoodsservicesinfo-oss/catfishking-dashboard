# Catfish King Operational Portal - FAQ
## Frequently Asked Questions

---

## General Questions

**Q: What is this new system?**
A: It's a replacement for DataCache — a digital portal where you submit daily reports directly to Corporate instead of printing and emailing spreadsheets.

**Q: Why are we changing?**
A: The new system saves time (no printing), gives Corporate real-time visibility, and you own the data (not a third party).

**Q: Is this mandatory?**
A: Yes, after full rollout. During pilot, you'll use both DataCache and the new system for 2 weeks to compare.

---

## Login & Access

**Q: I forgot my password**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page, or contact Corporate to reset.

**Q: Can I use this on my phone?**
A: Tablet or computer works best. Mobile browser works but isn't optimized.

**Q: Can multiple people login?**
A: Each store manager has their own login. Don't share passwords.

---

## Daily Sales Report

**Q: Do I still use the POS to get numbers?**
A: Yes! The POS gives you the numbers. The new system is where you enter them.

**Q: What if I make a mistake?**
A: You can edit and re-submit the same day. Contact Corporate if you need to change a previous day.

**Q: Why are some fields grayed out?**
A: Those are auto-calculated (like "Total" fields). The system adds Inside + Drive-Thru for you.

**Q: What if I don't have Drive-Thru?**
A: Enter 0 for Drive-Thru fields. (Note: Check with Corporate — some stores may not have Drive-Thru)

**Q: What if my shift times are different (not 2PM/4PM)?**
A: Use the closest times. Contact Corporate if this is an issue for your store.

---

## Data & Reports

**Q: Can Corporate see my data in real-time?**
A: Yes, as soon as you click "Submit" it appears on their dashboard.

**Q: Can other stores see my data?**
A: No. Store managers can only see their own store. Corporate can see all stores.

**Q: What happens to my old DataCache data?**
A: We're importing historical data so you can see trends going back 2 years.

**Q: Can I export my store's data?**
A: Yes, from your dashboard. You can export to Excel or PDF.

---

## Auto-Population (The Cool Stuff)

**Q: What's auto-population?**
A: When you submit Daily Sales, the system automatically fills other reports (Daily Log, Labor Projection) with your sales numbers. No re-entry!

**Q: Where does my sales data go?**
A: 
- Daily Sales → Daily Log (sales numbers)
- Daily Sales → Labor Projection (actual volume)
- Daily Sales → Weekly Inventory (net sales for COGS %)

**Q: Do I still need to fill out Daily Log?**
A: Yes, but only the non-sales parts (position chart, duty list, etc.). Sales numbers appear automatically.

## Technical Issues

**Q: The page isn't loading**
A: Try:
1. Refresh browser (Ctrl+R or Cmd+R)
2. Clear browser cache
3. Try different browser (Chrome works best)
4. Check internet connection

**Q: "Form submission failed" error**
A: Check:
- Required fields (marked with *) are filled
- Numbers only (no $ or commas) in number fields
- No negative numbers where not allowed

**Q: Calculations look wrong**
A: Double-check you entered Inside and Drive-Thru in the correct fields. Inside should be larger than Drive-Thru at most stores.

**Q: System is down**
A: Use your paper backup process. Enter data when system is back up. Contact Corporate if down more than 1 hour.
