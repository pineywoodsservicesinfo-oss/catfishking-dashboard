## Store Manager Quick Start

**Welcome to the Catfish King Operational Portal!**

This guide will walk you through using the new system for your daily reporting.

## Daily Workflow (5 Minutes)

### Step 1: Login
1. Go to: `https://portal.catfishking.com` (or your custom URL)
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click "Sign In"

**Forgot password?** Click "Reset Password" or contact Corporate.

### Step 2: Submit Daily Sales Report
1. From your dashboard, click **"Daily Sales Report"**
2. Select today's date (or the date you're reporting for)
3. Fill in the form sections:

#### Shift Readings
- Inside/Drive-Thru at 2 PM
- Inside/Drive-Thru at 4 PM
- Inside/Drive-Thru at Close

> 💡 **Tip:** Totals auto-calculate! Just fill in Inside and Drive-Thru.

#### Sales Breakdown
- Total Gross Sales
- Tax Exempt Sales
- Gift Certificates Redeemed
- Cash Paid Outs

#### Payment Methods
- Credit Card Sales
- Online Sales
- Delivery Sales
- Charge Sales

#### Cash/Deposits
- Cash Held Back on Friday (if applicable)
- Cash Put Back on Sunday (if applicable)
- Deposit #1, #2, #3

> 💡 **Tip:** Total Deposit auto-sums your 3 deposits.

#### Voids
- Inside Voids (count and dollars)
- Drive-Thru Voids (count and dollars)

#### Final Notes
- Gift Certificates Sold
- Special Deposit Dollars
- Comments (optional but helpful!)

### Step 3: Review & Submit
1. Scroll to bottom of form
2. Review totals (Inside + Drive-Thru = Total)
3. Click **"Submit Report"**
4. See confirmation message

✅ **Done!** Corporate can see your report instantly.

## What Happens Next?

### Auto-Filled Reports
When you submit Daily Sales, the system automatically fills:
- ✅ Daily Log (your sales numbers appear automatically)
- ✅ Labor Projection (actual volume tracked)
- ✅ Weekly Inventory (net sales for COGS %)

**You don't need to re-enter this data!**
